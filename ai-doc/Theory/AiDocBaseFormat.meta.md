# AiDocBaseFormat.meta.md

Specification for editing [AiDoc writing principles](AiDocBaseFormat.md). Defines purpose, usage patterns, and constraints.

## AiDocBaseFormat.md goals and principles

- Provides shared writing and formatting principles for documentation used by AI agents during practical tasks.
- Keeps broadly loaded guidance small: every addition affects many unrelated authoring tasks.
- Preserves the rationale for concise, unambiguous guidance without relying on guarantees about model attention, recall or tokenization.
- Leaves document structure to format specifications and task-specific procedure to workflows.
