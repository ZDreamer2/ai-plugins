# AiDocMetaDoc Theory and Context

## Two reading contexts

An agent using a document needs guidance for the practical task. An agent editing that document also needs to understand its purpose, audience, relationships and the reasons behind its boundaries.

Putting all of that authoring context into the practical document burdens every task that reads it. Leaving it only in the original conversation makes later editors reconstruct intent. A companion AiDocMetaDoc preserves that context between editing sessions while keeping it out of normal document use.

## Protection without freezing

A plausible rewrite can improve wording while losing an important distinction or expanding beyond the document's responsibility. The meta helps future editors recognize what would be lost and why it matters.

That protection applies to intent, not every existing decision. The target document remains free to evolve. A meta that mirrors its steps or preserves its entire current structure creates a second version to maintain rather than explaining what makes the document useful.

A meta can precede the target by recording its purpose and important constraints. It need not settle the future document's contents in advance. Where the format and task already supply enough context, a separate meta may add little value.

## Complementary responsibilities

- The AiDoc guides the agent doing the task.
- The AiDocFormatDoc defines the structure and writing standards for that kind of document.
- The AiDocMetaDoc explains what matters about this particular document and why, so future authors can apply the format with its intended use in mind.

The [AiDocMetaDoc format](../Formats/MetaDoc/AiDocMetaDoc.format.md) owns the content standard; the [meta creation workflow](../workflows/meta/do.md) owns the authoring process.
